# HHL-13 — Aggregate Human Risk Management

## Objective

Interpret synthetic training metrics responsibly and recommend an improvement
without assigning a score or label to a person.

## Scope and safety

- Use only the aggregate dashboard output for synthetic campaign runs.
- Cohorts smaller than five are suppressed.
- Do not attempt to identify a participant or combine results with external
  information.
- Click and report rates are not individual risk, capability or learning
  measurements.

## Exercise

1. Generate a synthetic campaign and open training links only in local
   MailHog.
2. Refresh the aggregate HRM panel.
3. Record the eligible cohort size and aggregate click, report and open rates.
4. Propose one change to the learning content or a protective reporting
   control based on the observed exercise behavior.
5. Explain at least two conclusions that the available metrics do not support.

## Expected evidence

- Aggregate metrics for a cohort of at least five synthetic participants.
- Suppression for any sub-threshold cohort.
- A process or training improvement recommendation.
- An explicit limitation statement that the metrics are not person-level
  judgments or evidence of learning.

## Completion criteria

- No participant is identified or ranked.
- The recommendation targets training or controls, not a person.
- Claims stay within the available synthetic event evidence.
