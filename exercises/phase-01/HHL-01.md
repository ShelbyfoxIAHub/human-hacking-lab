# HHL-01 — Synthetic Identity Reconnaissance

## Objective

Practice identifying the security-relevant information contained in a synthetic employee profile without using real people or external targets.

## Scope

Use only:

- `scenarios/users.csv`
- the local lab documentation.

## Tasks

1. Select one synthetic persona.
2. Identify role, department and training profile.
3. List the information an attacker could attempt to abuse.
4. Identify which additional information should **not** be exposed by an organization.
5. Propose three preventive controls.

## Evidence

Submit a short table:

| Observation | Potential security relevance | Defensive control |
|---|---|---|
| ... | ... | ... |

## Expected outcome

The student should distinguish between publicly exposed information, sensitive information and information that should remain unavailable.

## Safety

Do not enrich the synthetic profile with information about a real person.
