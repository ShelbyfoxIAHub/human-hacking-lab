# HHL-17 — Integrated Learning Path

## Objective

Use the prerequisite-aware curriculum to plan a safe sequence across the
lab's security tracks and review the evidence required at each step.

## Scope and safety

- Use a synthetic learner ID only.
- Progress markers stay in this browser and are self-reported.
- Do not enter real names, email addresses or other personal information.
- A completed marker is not a certificate or independent evidence review.

## Exercise

1. Open **Ruta curricular integrada** and review module prerequisites.
2. Begin with the foundations module and identify its required evidence.
3. Follow the available branches for Red Team, Detection Engineering and
   synthetic OSINT/AppSec.
4. Complete the Blue Team module before Threat Hunting/DFIR; complete the
   required branches before Human Risk Management, Security Engineering and
   governance.
5. Mark a module complete only after completing its exercise and reviewing
   the listed evidence criteria.
6. Change the synthetic learner ID and confirm that progress is separated by
   browser-local ID; use **Reiniciar progreso local** to clear a test path.

## Expected evidence

- A prerequisite-consistent route through the curriculum.
- Exercise artifacts cited for marked modules.
- Clear understanding that local completion is self-reported and not verified.

## Completion criteria

- No module is marked complete before its prerequisites.
- No real personal data is stored in the learner ID or local progress.
- The learner can explain the limits of self-reported progress.
